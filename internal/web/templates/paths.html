A path starts with a flaw in a service that answers, or with a service that needs no sign-in. A flaw in a library of an image or of a service counts as a way in only when it is known or likely to be exploited (CISA KEV, or EPSS from 0.10): nearly every image has some library flaw, and whether the app uses the flawed part is unknown. The others are listed under findings Varde cannot place. Varde cannot see router port forwards or firewalls. A path from the internet is based on proxy routes under your public domains; a flaw of the service itself whose attack vector is not published is assumed to be remote and marked as such.
