Supporting containers{{if $d.NotApps}} ({{len $d.NotApps}}){{end}}

Socket proxies, update helpers and images that others build on a database have no advisories of their own that Varde could compare, so they are not counted among the apps above. They are not skipped: the packages and libraries inside their images are checked like those of every container, and what is found is in the findings. PostgreSQL, Redis, Valkey, MariaDB, MySQL, MongoDB and Memcached in their official images are apps and are checked as such.
