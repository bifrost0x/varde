How a machine gets onto this list

A machine is listed once Varde can read something on it. That happens in four ways: Docker on this server; the agent on a host (every package and container); a platform you connect, such as Proxmox, UniFi or a NAS (its guests and its own version); or an app that tells its version to anyone who asks, the way Nextcloud does. The last one needs nothing from you, which is why a machine can appear after a scan alone while others do not.
